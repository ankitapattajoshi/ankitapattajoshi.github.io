MoodSync: an AI-powered, emotion-based music experience

Feel first, play next.

View on Behance
RoleUX/UI designer
TypeConcept case study
PlatformMobile app
FocusAI, biometrics, music

The problem

Streaming platforms have created a new kind of digital exhaustion. Users spend an average of 12.4 minutes a day just deciding what to listen to. This "search fatigue" breaks up the listening experience and turns a moment of emotional connection into a menu-scrolling task.

“I don’t just want songs that match my mood. I want music that helps me move through it.”User interview

The solution: harmony of tech and emotion

MoodSync removes the friction of choice. It reads your facial cues and turns them into the right soundscape, so discovery is instant and no search is needed. Every session is unique, reacting to your pulse, your environment and your immediate sentiment.

Detect emotion

Real-time scanning of micro-expressions.

AI analysis

Neural mapping of emotional data to harmonic signatures.

Personalised playlist

A playlist of tracks tailored to your state of mind.

0.8ssync time
99%detection accuracy

How it works

A proprietary emotional mapping engine turns biometric facial markers into a fluid listening environment in real time.

1. Capture face

High-frequency sampling of 128 key facial muscle points.

2. Detect emotion

Neural analysis maps micro-expressions to the VAD emotional scale.

3. AI processing

Algorithms match mood with spectral audio signatures.

4. Playlist generation

A living playlist adapts tempo and key to your state.

The emotive transition

MoodSync doesn't only match your mood. It guides you toward a better state through curated auditory bridges, moving from a sad track, through a neutral middle, to a happier one.

94.2%growth in emotional velocity
42 minper session

Understanding the audience

Research pointed to three user profiles. Mapping their emotional journeys shaped an interface built for deep work, daily utility and musical exploration.

The Academic

Graduate student“I need an environment where silence isn’t empty, but filled with a texture that helps me focus.”

Needs:

  • Deep focus
  • Lo-fi textures

Behaviours:

  • 4+ hour study sessions
  • Uses generative noise to block distractions
  • Prefers a dark UI to reduce eye strain

The High-Achiever

Product lead“Music is my only transition between ‘Work Mode’ and ‘Home Mode’.”

Needs:

  • Efficiency
  • Stress decompression
  • Quick discovery

Behaviours:

  • Listens on high-speed commutes
  • Curates “Power Playlists” for workouts
  • Demands seamless cross-device syncing

The Audiophile

Sound designer“I listen to the space between notes. The UI should be as elegant as the soundscape.”

Needs:

  • High fidelity
  • Deep exploration
  • Spatial audio

Behaviours:

  • Active listening, no multitasking
  • Digs for rare independent recordings
  • Values visual aesthetics as much as audio

User journey flow

Five steps from opening the app to a playlist that keeps adapting: open app with biometric sign-in, scan face to read emotional state, detect mood from the core frequency, generate playlist from mood and taste, then listen and adapt as feedback adjusts the audio.

Precision AI scanning

Real-time facial topography mapping analyses emotional markers on the device screen, with clear status, data acquisition progress and an option to recalibrate.

12mslatency
3,402data points

Because the scan is sensitive, the screen states that it is end-to-end encrypted, and a neural config panel lets users tune sensitivity and fidelity.

Generated playlists

Playlists are built for your emotional state and use real-time biometric feedback to move your listening from stress toward serenity.

-24%average cognitive load
98%match rate

Adaptive learning loops

MoodSync doesn't just play tracks. It learns your intent, and every interaction refines the soundscape in real time.

The feedback loop

Continuous signal processing through user sentiment markers.

Transition mismatch

A skip is read as friction and triggers an immediate shift in the melodic structure.

Mood reinforcement

Likes act as anchors, stabilising the generative parameters and deepening the current mood.

Design foundations

The visual language of emotional intelligence: each colour stands for an emotional state.

Typography

UI modules and atoms

Primary glow and glass-morph buttons, icon controls, progress bars and track cards that share one system.

Dynamic emotional UI

An adaptive colour engine analyses the tone of the music in real time and shifts the interface's colour temperature to reflect the user's emotional state and the playback environment.

Challenges and considerations

Working where machine intelligence meets human emotion meant designing with care.

Emotion accuracy

Interpreting emotions is complex, so the product has to stay humble about what it detects.

Privacy concerns

Biometric data is sensitive, which is why scanning states and encryption are shown clearly.

User trust

People need confidence in AI decisions, so users can recalibrate and give feedback.

Future vision

The next frontiers for MoodSync are emotional intelligence and biometric harmony.

Voice emotion detection

Understanding emotion from tone and speech patterns.

Smartwatch integration

Syncing your heart rate with the rhythm of the soundscape.

AI learning

Building a unique profile that evolves with your listening habits.

Takeaway

MoodSync is an experience that listens to you. The aim was to cut the effort of choosing, and to make a music app feel closer to a companion that understands how you feel.
